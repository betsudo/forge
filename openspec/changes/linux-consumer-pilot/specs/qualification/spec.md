# Native and hosted evidence boundaries

### Requirement: Native Linux premises

#### Scenario: Unsupported host or changed inputs
**WHEN** the stated condition occurs, Forge SHALL enforce the following.
**THEN** Mac, ARM and explicit emulation refuse before scratch or subprocesses. Changed
compiler/tool/source/parameter bytes cannot reuse qualification. A failed native
baseline/proof never produces a successful receipt or deletes unrelated data.

### Requirement: Consumer recovery

#### Scenario: Reconstructed artifacts remain usable
**WHEN** the stated condition occurs, Forge SHALL enforce the following.
**THEN** Run existing corruption, missing-export, explicit repair, eviction and concurrent
caller controls with fresh actual proofs and assertions. Add damaged final
compiler-output integrity refusal and explicit reconstruction before fresh proofs.

### Requirement: Honest CI collection

#### Scenario: Missing or failed job
**WHEN** the stated condition occurs, Forge SHALL enforce the following.
**THEN** Retain every expected job and attempt. Missing, cancelled, failed, duplicate,
overlapping-stage, identity-mismatched or byte-unmeasured evidence prevents a
savings report. Keep refusal evidence exportable. Synthetic local tests are
marked synthetic and never accepted as hosted measurements.
